# Weekly organic authority operations

## One-operator cadence

Run `npm run authority:generate`, then work from `generated/authority/{project}/weekly-execution.json`. The generator proposes a sustainable maximum: two legitimate profile/citation actions, two relationship actions, two useful social distributions, one editorial asset, one citation recheck, and one Search Console review. A missing evidence gate means verify rather than publish; it does not justify substituting a low-quality target.

### Monday — evidence and priorities
- Review target ownership, audience, moderation, topical/UAE relevance, contact route and entitlement.
- Promote a target to `verified` only with a real domain, submission URL and verification date. Human approval remains separate.
- Reconfirm mutable project facts against the approved source of truth.

### Tuesday–Wednesday — useful distribution
- Approve and publish at most two useful social pieces through verified business accounts; confirm image rights.
- Tailor one-to-one outreach for no more than two verified recipients. Never scrape or mass-send.
- Prepare up to two relevant, moderated profile/citation actions. A submission is not a citation.

### Thursday — authority asset
- Tailor one source-noted editorial angle to a real audience. Describe Finding Stories material as an advisory resource, not independent news.
- Preserve the project canonical and generated UTM; verify the project CTA still leads to WhatsApp/form conversion.

### Friday — evidence and revenue review
- Open every claimed placement. Record a citation as `live` only when the public URL, destination and context are visible; otherwise use `pending`, `changed`, `removed` or `unreachable` accurately.
- In Search Console record index state, impressions, queries, clicks, CTR and average position. Florence's prior state was **Discovered – currently not indexed**; this is not presently a technical failure. Do not repeatedly request indexing and do not use the Indexing API for an ordinary property page.
- Review referral/organic sessions, WhatsApp clicks, enquiry starts, successful leads and—where existing reporting supports them—meetings and EOI/bookings. Qualified enquiries are the primary KPI.

Keep a dated operator record outside generated files for actions and analytics. Generation deliberately leaves analytics unknown and resets no operational evidence.
