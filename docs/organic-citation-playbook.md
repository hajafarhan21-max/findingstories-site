# Legitimate citation playbook

## Gate and workflow
1. A human verifies publisher ownership, audience relevance, editorial standards, current contact details and entitlement to list Finding Stories/project facts.
2. Select manifest-generated copy, compare every fact with the source-of-truth notes, and edit for the audience without adding claims.
3. Use a branded/natural anchor, direct production URL and standard UTM. Record owner, placement URL, date, wording and review date. Verify the live page manually; never report an earned link before it is live.
4. Nofollow is acceptable: qualified discovery and accurate citation matter more than link attributes. Reject paid-follow arrangements and bulk submission offers.

## Category matrix
| Category | Acceptable / quality criteria | Avoid | Required verification | Anchor and destination | UTM medium | Length / duplication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company/business profiles | Real, moderated profile with accurate legal/contact fields | Fake address, clone directories | Business owner and profile entitlement | `Finding Stories`; homepage | `directory` | 80–160 words; customize |
| Property-industry directories | UAE/property relevance, indexed editorial pages, transparent operator | Mass/low-quality listings | Audience, moderation, fact source | Project name or branded guide; project when project-specific | `directory` | 60–150; avoid identical syndication |
| UAE business listings | Genuine business category and verified details | Invented office/service area | Legal/contact/location evidence | Brand; homepage | `directory` | Platform limit; unique intro |
| Professional profiles | A real team member controls the profile | Fabricated people/credentials | Identity, role, approval | Brand or descriptive guide; contextual destination | `referral` | 40–100; first-person review |
| Developer/project references | Accurate resource where inclusion is permitted | Claiming official representation | Written relationship/permission and current facts | `Azizi Florence buyer guide`; project | `partner` | 50–150; highest fact scrutiny |
| Editorial/resource pages | Standalone reader value and named editor | Paid-link farming, scraped copy | Editorial fit and disclosure rules | Natural descriptive phrase; project | `editorial` | Bespoke pitch; no duplicate article |
| Social/company profiles | Verified account controlled by business | Creating unapproved handles | Account ownership | Brand/project; context-dependent | `organic_social` | Native length; human approval |
| Partner pages | A real documented relationship | Logo swaps or invented partnership | Both parties approve wording | Brand/project; relevant page | `partner` | 50–120; relationship-specific |
| Media/PR | Newsworthy, fact-checked editor workflow | Fake coverage or guaranteed placement | Editor identity and claims | Brand/project guide | `press` | Use 50/100/200-word kit |
| Community/event pages | Genuine participation and audience fit | Irrelevant event spam | Attendance/organizer permission | Event context; homepage/project | `event` | 40–100; unique event facts |

Never automate submissions. Approved external links remain empty until verification.
