# Organic outreach kit

Replace brackets, verify the recipient and facts, send individually, and stop after one polite follow-up. Never imply a relationship, approval or coverage.

## Developer relationship
**Subject:** Accurate project information for Finding Stories readers

Hello [name] — Finding Stories has prepared a buyer-focused page for [project]. Could we confirm the appropriate source/contact for current public facts and discuss whether a legitimate resource mention is useful? We will not describe ourselves as an official representative without written approval.

## Project information request
Could you share the currently approved public fact sheet for [project], including pricing date, payment milestones, property mix and completion guidance? We will attribute the verification date and reconfirm changing terms.

## Editorial inclusion
Hello [editor] — Your coverage of [specific topic] may benefit from a concise, reviewed resource on [project/location]. We can provide 50/100/200-word factual summaries. No guaranteed-return or scarcity claims; editorial control remains yours.

## Local property publication
We have a buyer-oriented briefing on [project] in [location], covering verified residence types and questions buyers should confirm. May I send the review pack for consideration by the relevant editor?

## UAE resource page
I noticed [specific resource]. If useful to its readers, our [project] guide adds verified project context and a clear availability disclaimer. Would you consider it after editorial review?

## Broker/referral collaboration
Would you be open to discussing a transparent, documented collaboration for clients whose brief fits [project]? We would agree roles, permissions, disclosures and referral terms before any public mention.

## Event/exhibition mention
Finding Stories plans to [verified participation]. May we provide accurate listing copy and the relevant project/homepage URL for the official event page? Please confirm organizer requirements first.

## Community feature
Your audience in [community] may value a fact-safe overview of [relevant subject]. Could we share a tailored summary for review, without promotional urgency or unverified investment claims?

## Social collaboration
Would a jointly reviewed educational post on [buyer question] be relevant? We can supply verified inputs; nothing will publish until both parties approve copy, attribution and links.
