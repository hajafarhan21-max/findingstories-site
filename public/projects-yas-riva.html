THE PROJECT

Project overview.

Yas Riva is Aldar’s collection of 151 four-to-six-bedroom canal-front and canal-accessible villas on the northern shore of Yas Island.

Canal-front villas include private docks; canal-accessible villas connect to the waterfront by dedicated walkways. Aldar also publishes optional outdoor pods for uses such as a gym, office or majlis.

AMENITIES & LIFESTYLE

Designed around everyday life.

  • Meditation area and yoga studio

  • Adult and children’s pools

LOCATION

Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi

On the northern shore of Yas Island, the community sits within the island’s entertainment, retail, leisure and golf destination.

  • Yas Waterworld
  • Ferrari World Abu Dhabi
  • SeaWorld Abu Dhabi
  • Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi
  • Yas Marina Circuit
  • Yas Mall
  • Etihad Arena
  • 10 minutes to Zayed International Airport
  • 30 minutes to downtown Abu Dhabi
  • 1 hour to Dubai
