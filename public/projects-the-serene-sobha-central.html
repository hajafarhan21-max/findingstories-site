THE PROJECT

Project overview.

The Serene is a one- and two-bedroom tower within Sobha Central, designed around expansive glazing, skyline views and a landscaped podium.

Sobha publishes multiple one- and two-bedroom layouts with balconies, ranging from 495.14 to 872.20 sq ft.

AMENITIES & LIFESTYLE

Designed around everyday life.

  • Elevated urban park
  • Lap, leisure and children’s pools
  • Jacuzzi and outdoor exercise space
  • Jogging track and mini golf
  • Outdoor cinema and event lawn
  • Indoor gym, sauna and steam room
  • Children’s play areas and family barbecue spaces
  • Boutique retail and commercial clubhouse

LOCATION

Jebel Ali, Dubai

At Sobha Central on Sheikh Zayed Road, the project has a dedicated podium-level connection towards the road network and views across Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Islands, Bluewaters and Palm Jumeirah.
